DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

Federal Aviation Administration

[FAA-2026-12808]


Notice of Intent To Prepare a Programmatic Environmental Impact 
Statement (PEIS), Open Public Scoping Period, and Hold Public Scoping 
Meetings on the Proposed SpaceX Starship-Super Heavy Launch and Landing 
Operations at the Boca Chica Launch Site at Starbase, Texas and 
Expansion of Starship Landing and Expenditure Locations

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), Department of 
Transportation (DOT).

ACTION: Update regarding the notice of intent to prepare a PEIS, 
extending the public scoping period, and holding public scoping 
meetings.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the National Environmental Policy Act of 
1969, as amended (NEPA) and FAA Order 1050.1G, FAA National 
Environmental Policy Act Implementing Procedures, the FAA is providing 
an update regarding its notice intent to prepare a PEIS analyzing Space 
Exploration Technologies Corp.'s (SpaceX) proposal to increase 
Starship-Super Heavy operations at the Boca Chica Launch Site at 
Starbase, TX and expand Starship landing and expenditure locations. 
Subsequent license modifications or renewals under 14 CFR part 400 are 
within the scope of the PEIS, and the issuance of temporary airspace 
closures associated with the Proposed Action are included in FAA's 
Federal Action. FAA is requesting comments concerning the alternatives 
or impacts and on relevant information, studies, or analyses with 
respect to the proposed agency action. A Draft PEIS will be released 
for public review and comment at a later date.

DATES: The FAA will hold five virtual public meetings to solicit 
comments from the public concerning the scope and content of the EIS 
(dates and times below). Interested parties must register to join the 
public meetings. Registration information is available on the FAA's 
project website: <a href="https://www.faa.gov/space/stakeholder_engagement/spacex_starship">https://www.faa.gov/space/stakeholder_engagement/spacex_starship</a>.

<bullet> Meeting #2: Tuesday, November 17, 2026: 5:30-7:30 p.m. (CT)
<bullet> Meeting #3: Wednesday, November 18, 2026: 5:30-7:30 p.m. (CT)
<bullet> Meeting #4: Thursday, November 19, 2026: 1:00-3:00 p.m. (CT)

    A Notice of Intent (NOI) was released on October 14, 2026, which 
advertised both in-person and virtual scoping meetings. Please be 
advised that all public scoping meetings, including those previously 
scheduled for November 17 and 19, 2026 for the PEIS for Increased 
Launch/Landing Operations at Boca Chica Launch Site at Starbase, Texas 
and Expansion of Starship Landing and Expenditure Locations will now be 
conducted virtually. The FAA remains committed to keeping communities 
informed and providing opportunities for interested stakeholders to 
learn about the proposed project and provide input. Due to this change, 
the public comment period will be extended to December 7, 2026.
